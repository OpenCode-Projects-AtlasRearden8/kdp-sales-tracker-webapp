    Import & export

    Bring in a CSV you built by hand or downloaded from your KDP reports — the importer looks for date, title, format, marketplace, units, and royalty columns in any order. Export gives you a portable backup of every entry.

    Tip: the importer can append to what you already have, or replace everything — handy when a monthly KDP report overlaps entries from last month.
